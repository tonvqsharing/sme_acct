## Goal
Build Phase 3 — Tax Foundation on top of Phase 1 (Accounting Foundation) and Phase 2 (Accounting Control & Posting Configuration). Create a correct, configurable, traceable Vietnamese tax foundation: Tax Type, Tax Code, Tax Rule/Rate with effective dating, Tax Profile/Applicability, Tax Authority, Tax Period, Tax Accounting Mapping, Tax Exemption/Non-taxable Reason, and Tax Rule Version/Legal Traceability. Must integrate with COA, Posting Configuration, Currency, and Accounting Period from prior phases. Regulatory research against current Vietnamese tax legislation required. TDD. No tax calculation engine yet — foundation only.
